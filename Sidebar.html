How this works

Generate the monthly queue from the New Hires tab. Find photo candidates from Gmail and optional Slack profile photos. HR approves one photo, then the app creates the employee welcome slide and updates the collage.

The app does not send employee emails and does not automate a browser. Gmail search, Slack lookup, and Slides edits are explicit HR actions.
